## 1. Who we are and when this notice applies
FleetIQ is fleet-management software supplied by Ian Crofts trading as FleetIQ. This notice explains how FleetIQ handles personal information where FleetIQ determines the purposes of processing, including account administration, service security, support, supplier/customer administration and operation of the FleetIQ service.

A subscribing customer organisation will normally be the controller for driver, employee, contractor and operational records that it enters into FleetIQ. For that customer-controlled information, FleetIQ normally acts as processor and processes it to provide the service under the customer agreement and Data Processing Addendum (DPA). Questions about why an employer or fleet operator uses a person's information should normally be directed to that organisation first.

## 2. Information we may handle
Depending on use of the service, information may include names; work and contact details; account identifiers; organisation membership and permissions; driver/licence and compliance details; vehicle assignments; inspections; defects; repairs and workshop activity; uploaded documents/evidence; audit and security records; support communications; and technical information needed to operate and protect the service.

FleetIQ does not require customers to provide information that is unnecessary for their fleet-management purpose. Customers are responsible for deciding what customer-controlled personal information they enter and for ensuring they have a lawful basis to do so.

## 3. Why FleetIQ uses personal information as controller
FleetIQ may process information to create and administer accounts and subscriptions; authenticate users and secure the service; provide support and respond to enquiries; prevent misuse and investigate security incidents; maintain business, contractual and accounting records; manage legal/privacy requests; and comply with legal obligations.

The lawful basis depends on the activity. FleetIQ expects to rely principally on performance of a contract or steps requested before a contract, legitimate interests in operating and securing a commercial SaaS service, and compliance with legal obligations. Where another basis is required for a particular activity, FleetIQ will identify it at the relevant point. FleetIQ does not rely on consent where consent is not the appropriate lawful basis.

## 4. Customer-controlled fleet information
Where FleetIQ acts as processor, it processes customer personal data on the customer's documented instructions, including the instructions contained in the customer agreement and DPA. The customer determines the purposes for which its driver, employee and fleet records are used and is responsible for its own privacy information and lawful basis.

## 5. Sharing and subprocessors
Access within a customer account is controlled by that customer's authorised users and permissions. FleetIQ may use vetted service providers where necessary to host, secure, communicate or support the service. The current confirmed production subprocessor register is published with FleetIQ's legal information. Supabase is currently the confirmed infrastructure provider used for the central database, authentication, storage and server-side functions. Additional providers will not be presented as confirmed subprocessors until selected and assessed.

## 6. International transfers
The location and transfer position of each relevant subprocessor is recorded in the Subprocessor Register. Where personal information is transferred outside the UK and UK data-protection law requires a safeguard, FleetIQ will use an applicable lawful transfer mechanism and document it before that processing is introduced.

## 7. Retention
FleetIQ keeps information only for as long as needed for the relevant purpose, customer instructions, contractual commitments, dispute/security requirements and applicable law. Customer-controlled operational data is handled under the customer agreement, DPA and FleetIQ Retention & Deletion Policy. Ending a subscription does not cause uncontrolled immediate deletion: an authorised offboarding process is used so export, legal holds, audit requirements and backup expiry can be handled safely.

## 8. Your rights
Depending on the circumstances, individuals may have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and data portability. Rights are not absolute in every case. Requests can be submitted through FleetIQ's Legal & Privacy Centre or to privacy@fleetiq.org.uk. FleetIQ may need to verify identity. If FleetIQ is acting only as processor for the relevant data, FleetIQ will normally coordinate the request with the customer controller.

## 9. Complaints
Please contact privacy@fleetiq.org.uk first so the issue can be investigated. Individuals also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO). Current ICO contact and complaint information is available from the ICO's official website.

## 10. Security and changes
FleetIQ uses organisation-scoped tenancy, authenticated access, row-level security, role/permission controls, tenant-scoped document storage paths and controlled recovery mechanisms. No internet service can promise absolute security. FleetIQ reviews this notice and will publish a new version when material changes require it; where appropriate, FleetIQ will require users to acknowledge or accept a new version.
