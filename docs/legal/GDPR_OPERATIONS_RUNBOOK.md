# FleetIQ GDPR Operations Runbook — Production Candidate v2026-09-15.2

**Privacy lead/contact:** Ian Crofts — privacy@fleetiq.org.uk  
**Postal contact:** 20 Tunstall Drive, Nottingham, NG5 1LZ, United Kingdom

1. Record every privacy/rights request with requesting user, organisation, request type, received date and details. Verify identity proportionately before disclosure or material account changes.
2. Decide whether FleetIQ is controller or processor for the affected information. For customer-controlled fleet/driver data, coordinate with the customer controller unless law requires otherwise.
3. Scope only the correct tenant and records. Never export or disclose another organisation's data. Preserve an audit trail of searches, decisions and actions.
4. Assess the applicable right, lawful basis, exemptions and retention/legal-hold requirements. Record the decision and response deadline.
5. Export, rectify, restrict or delete only the approved scope. Where deletion is approved, include relevant downstream systems and allow protected backups to expire under the documented lifecycle.
6. Record completion, communications and any refusal/partial refusal rationale. Escalate complaints, suspected breaches and complex cases for qualified legal/data-protection advice where appropriate.
7. For offboarding, verify administrator/owner authority; confirm export requirement; revoke access; execute documented deletion/return; retain only justified restricted records; and record completion.
8. For a suspected personal-data breach, contain the incident, preserve evidence, determine affected tenants/data, assess risk promptly, notify affected customer controllers without undue delay where FleetIQ is processor, and assess FleetIQ's own ICO/data-subject notification duties where FleetIQ is controller.
9. Review subprocessors, retention settings and privacy information whenever production suppliers or processing purposes materially change.
